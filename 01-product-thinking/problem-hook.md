# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** They will likely lose market share. it appears the way their solution is designed, it takes significant amount of extra work for operators to be able to use it compared to their competition.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** They are probably maintaining routes and driver assignments to lanes and assets outside the tool in spreadsheets, and communicating plans via email.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** If the company does not simplify their software to reduce administrative overhead and enable quick, easy use towards execution, their user base is inclined to either make their own way or look at alternate solutions that are easier to maintain, update and use for their day to day activities.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** The value is to deliver a leaner product that focuses on maintaining/enhancing the tool's high end enterprise capabilities, restoring the core value while stripping down any distractions, extra clicks and any other administrative overhead (like manual master data maintenance etc..)
